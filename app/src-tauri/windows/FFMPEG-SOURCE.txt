Backsight ships FFmpeg 8.1.3 (GPL) and the libraries it is built with, as the avcodec, avdevice,
avfilter, avformat, avutil, swresample and swscale DLLs in this folder. FFMPEG-BUILDINFO.txt
lists the exact configuration; the LICENSE-*.txt files hold every library's license text.

The complete corresponding source code (every source archive, pinned by SHA-256, plus the build
script) is published as ffmpeg-8.1.3-backsight-slim-win64-gpl-shared-sources.tar on the release
ffmpeg-8.1.3-slim-r1 of https://github.com/backsight-app/backsight and kept for at least three years
after the last Backsight release that ships this build.

Backsight itself is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later
(LICENSE.txt); its source is at the same address.
